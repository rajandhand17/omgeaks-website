# OmGeaks Brand Guidelines

## Brand Essence

**OmGeaks** engineers future digital experiences. The brand sits at the intersection of luxury product design and frontier technology — precise, calm, powerful.

Personality: Luxury · Minimal · Modern · Premium · Powerful · Elegant · High-end · Technology · Future · Innovation

---

## Logo Concept

**Digital Infinity × Neural Hexagon**

The mark combines:
- A hexagonal frame (precision, systems, enterprise structure)
- An infinity loop (continuity, scale, digital transformation)
- Neural nodes (AI, intelligence, connected systems)

It is not letterforms in a box. It is a memorable geometric icon usable from favicon to billboard.

## Clear Space

Maintain clear space equal to **½ the icon width** on all sides. Never crowd the mark with other UI or typography closer than this.

Minimum digital size:
- Icon alone: **24×24 px**
- Horizontal lockup: **120 px wide**

## Logo Colors

| Token | Hex | Role |
|-------|-----|------|
| Primary Cyan | `#00F5FF` | Energy, tech signal |
| Secondary Violet | `#7B5CFF` | Depth, intelligence |
| Accent Mint | `#5EFCE8` | Soft highlight |
| Highlight | `#FFFFFF` | Typography / nodes |
| Background | `#050505` | Primary canvas |
| Glass Border | `rgba(255,255,255,.08)` | Surfaces |

Gradient direction: top-left → bottom-right (cyan → violet → mint).

---

## Incorrect Use

- Do not place letters inside a box as the logo
- Do not stretch, rotate, or add drop shadows to the mark
- Do not recolor nodes independently of the brand gradient
- Do not place the dark logo on dark backgrounds without contrast adjustment
- Do not outline the infinity stroke with competing brand colors

---

## Typography

| Role | Recommendation | Fallback |
|------|-----------------|----------|
| Display / Headlines | **Syne** (Bold / ExtraBold) | system-ui |
| Body / UI | **Outfit** (Regular / Medium) | system-ui |
| Mono / Code accents | **JetBrains Mono** | monospace |

Headline style: massive, tight tracking slightly open (`letter-spacing: -0.02em` to `-0.04em`), generous line-height on multi-line, short copy.

Body: minimal paragraphs, high legibility, muted white (`rgba(255,255,255,0.65)`).

---

## Motion Language

- Easing: luxury cubic (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`)
- Prefer GPU-composited transforms and opacity
- Hover: magnetic pull, liquid borders, glow bloom
- Scroll: cinematic section reveals, parallax depth
- Never chaotic or playful — motion should feel expensive and inevitable

---

## Voice

Confident. Precise. Sparse. Never startup-hype. Speak like a studio that ships products worth seven figures.
